How to play

Your job: keep the last two dodos alive.

    Controls

    WASDDRAGSteer him. Right thumb, anywhere on screen.Or arrow keys. He goes where you point… mostly.
    SPACEHUSHHold to hush. Freezes him. Inside a bush, he hides.
    SHIFTHONKHonk. Snaps him out of any distraction and makes him sprint. Loud.
    FJUMPJump. Hops rocks, can clear a lunge. Sometimes he eats it.
    MOUSE2 FINGERSCamera. Drag to spin, scroll to zoom.Spin and pinch. Compass re-centers.

    Survival tips

    • The T-rex needs to see him. Under a cluster of trees (or HUSH under one) he's invisible. Running gives him away.
    • The nest grove is safe. Nothing big ever comes in.
    • Doda follows anything in his beak. That's how you get her home.
    • If it goes for Doda, HONK near it. He's the decoy now.
    • When it lunges, go sideways. It turns like a bus.
